# GLOBAL Signature — design review

November 17, 2026. Owner direction: balance private-club atmosphere with modern finance. Photography commercial-use permission confirmed by owner before asset retrieval.

## Changed experience

- Photo-led event and series openings with edition mastheads and a serif editorial type hierarchy.
- Clear event facts, two numbered day chapters, larger imagery and varied section proportions.
- Golf uses warm ivory and course imagery; finance uses navy, gold, and structured program themes.
- Native modal venue gallery with next/previous controls, keyboard navigation, Escape/close and focus restoration.
- Unified two-person package presentation, full/split price comparison and preserved payment gates.
- Registration stays closed and is summarized cleanly; its disabled form preview remains accessible through a disclosure.
- Updated imagery in the existing homepage Invitational feature and initiative cards. Shared brand and unrelated divisions preserved.
- Mobile gallery scrolling, stacked content, compact booking shortcuts, named icon links and scoped solid header contrast.

## Reviewed locally

Desktop at 1440 × 1000 and mobile at 390 × 844. No horizontal overflow on event or series pages. Gallery opening on the requested photograph, next navigation and closing were exercised. Course facts, lodging limits, future-city limits, editorial Summit-image disclosure, public email, charity gate and checkout gates remain present.
